# Team position in the jam

Facts specific to this team. General rules are in `rules-digest.md`.

## Eligibility

- All three members live in Argentina, which is an Eligible Jurisdiction with no prize restriction.
- No member works for Epic or a partner.
- All three are students, so the entry also qualifies as a Student Submission. Each member must be enrolled at a higher-education institution at submission time.
- Team Owner and prize payee: Nicolas.

## Prizes in reach

- Main: Best Overall (Unreal Engine) or Best Student Project. A student entry is considered for both and can win only one.
- Modifier, at most one: Best Team, Render Royalty or Glass Box.
- Development Continuation Grants are by invitation and separate from prizes.

## Strategy notes

- AI use earns no points by itself. The harness pays off through speed and through the Glass Box story.
- Sourced assets are not scored. Spend original and AI-generated effort on what players see most.
- Ties are re-scored with double weight on Fun Factor.
